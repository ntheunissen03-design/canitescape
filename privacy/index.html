Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Can It Escape? ("the game") is made to be played without an account. This page explains what the game does with information.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (levels, stars, best times, settings and purchases you've made) is saved on your device only. The game also keeps a small log of gameplay events (for example "level 7 won in 18 seconds") on your device.

Game settings from our website

When it starts, the game downloads a small settings file from our website (ntheunissen03-design.github.io/canitescape, hosted by GitHub) so we can adjust things like how many seconds a rescue gives. This request contains no personal information. The game assigns itself a random identifier, stored only on your device, to place you in groups for tests of these settings.

Ads

The free game may show ads through AppLovin, our ad partner. If you allow tracking when your phone asks, AppLovin may use your device's advertising identifier to show more relevant ads and measure them; if you don't, ads are not personalised. AppLovin's own privacy policy explains what it collects: applovin.com/privacy. Buying "No ads" removes ads between levels.

Analytics

The game does not send gameplay data anywhere today. If we turn on analytics in a later version to understand, in aggregate, how far players get and where levels are too hard, it will send gameplay events (for example "level 7 won in 18 seconds", frame rate and error messages) with a random identifier to our own server. They will never include your name, contacts, location or photos, will be used only to improve the game, and this page will be updated first.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple (or Google). We never see your payment details.

Notifications

If you allow them, the game schedules a reminder for the Daily Ring on your phone. These are created on your device; no server sends them.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Sharing clips

When you tap "share clip", the game saves a video of your round on your phone and opens the share sheet. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to share it.
